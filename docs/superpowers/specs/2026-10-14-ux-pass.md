# UX pass: phone first, install, and the audit's top fixes (2026-10-14)

**Sources:**
- The user's request: "improve UI/UX; make sure everything is smooth for people playing on a phone, and make sure Add to Home Screen works."
- A 5-lens audit (first-time player, phone, pad, visual, feel) with screenshots under the scratchpad `ux/` folder.
- Chrome `Page.getInstallabilityErrors` on the live site returns `[]`, with a valid manifest and an active, controlling service worker. Install already works on Android and desktop Chrome, and nothing here may break it.

**Global rules:**
- Zero dependencies.
- No `Math.random` or `Date.now` in `src/game`.
- Optional modules stay guarded.
- `npm test` (never `node --test tests/`) stays green, and the sim gates in `tests/sim.test.js` are not loosened.
- Keyboard and mouse behaviour changes only where this spec says so.
- Every builder verifies visually: it takes screenshots, looks at them, and checks phone metrics where relevant.

- new `tests/install.test.js`

### T1. Tap to shoot where you tap

In sticks mode, a short tap on the right half (under 180 ms and under 6 internal px of travel) fires at the tapped point, as a mouse click would.

- For that tap, `input.sample()` sets `aimDirX` and `aimDirY` to `null` and `aimX`/`aimY` to the tap point, with `fire: true`, for the tap's duration plus about 120 ms.
- Hold and drag behaves exactly as today.
- Mouse, pad and bot inputs are unchanged.

### T2. Sticks you can understand

- **Ghost rings:** idle rings are drawn at about 0.3 alpha (now 0.18).
- **First-run labels:** until each stick has been used once (stored with `CB.Save` under `touchTaught`, guarded), its ghost ring carries a label, `MOVE` or `AIM + FIRE`. The label pulses at 1 Hz or less, steady in calm mode.

### T3. Thumbs that never go dead

`setMode(st, 'sticks')`, coming back from a menu or pause, re-grabs every touch pointer still held down as a fresh stick, with its origin at the finger's current position. It no longer marks it ignored. The plain `menu → sticks` change at a scene start is unchanged for new pointers.

### T4. Controls in the side bars

On wide phones, the black side bars are where thumbs rest.

**Full-resolution pass:**
- After the letterboxed blit, `render.js` gets a second drawing pass at full canvas resolution for the touch overlay.
- When each side bar is at least about 40 game px wide (in game-pixel units at the current scale), the idle rings, DASH, /c and II are anchored in the bars:
  - move on the left;
  - aim, DASH and /c on the right;
  - II at the top of the right bar.
- Every anchor respects the **safe-area insets** (the iPhone notch and home indicator). Read them from CSS `env(safe-area-inset-*)` through a probe element, and refresh them on resize.

**Rules in every layout:**
- Live rings and knobs follow the real finger: no clamped, detached ring.
- Touch hit areas match where things are drawn.
- With no bars (16:9 screens), keep today's in-arena layout.

Pixel-art look: integer-scaled chunky rings drawn at the canvas scale, never smoothed.

### T5. Install

**`src/core/install.js` (`CB.Install`):**
- On load, listen for `beforeinstallprompt` (call `preventDefault` and stash the event) and `appinstalled`.
- API:
  - `kind()` returns `'prompt'` (the stashed event exists), `'ios'` (iPhone or iPad Safari, not standalone), or `null` (already installed or standalone, or unsupported).
  - `prompt()` resolves to `'accepted'`, `'dismissed'` or `'unavailable'` and never throws.
  - `standalone()` checks `matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)')`, `(display-mode: fullscreen)` and `navigator.standalone`.
  - `showIosHelp()` / `hideIosHelp()` toggle a DOM overlay (`#install-help` in `index.html`): a centred card with a share icon drawn in CSS or inline SVG and the text "Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen". Tapping it hides it.

**`index.html` head:**
- `apple-mobile-web-app-title` set to `Clawd`
- `apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style` set to `black-translucent`

**`manifest.webmanifest`:**
- add `"id": "/"`
- `short_name` becomes `"Clawd"` (home-screen labels truncate at about 12 characters)
- keep `display: fullscreen` (iOS falls back to standalone)

Re-run the CDP installability check on the built site (headless Chrome `Page.getInstallabilityErrors` must stay `[]`).

### T6. Portrait overlay

- A bigger rotate icon and the line "Turn your device sideways to play".
- A second, smaller line: "Rotation locked? Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center."

## F: flow and screens

Owns:
- `src/game/stageRunner.js`, `src/scenes/play.js`, `src/scenes/victory.js`, `src/scenes/patchPick.js`, `src/scenes/gameOver.js`, `src/scenes/screens.js`
- `tests/stageRunner.test.js`, `tests/sim.test.js` (only timing expectations, never the gates), `tests/play.devices.test.js`, `tests/scenes.test.js`, `tests/draw.screens.test.js`, `tests/share.test.js`, `tests/unlocks.test.js`, `tests/menus.devices.test.js` (cases for these screens only)

### F1. Read the BUG FIXED card

- `T.bossDead` goes from 3.0 to about 4.6 s, so the finished card holds about 1.7 s.
- A confirm press after the `✓ 1 test passing` line has shown skips ahead.
- A retry of a boss already seen keeps 3.0 s.

### F2. The victory receipt

- **Framed buttons:** two framed buttons, `[ share result ]` and `[ continue ]`. On touch they are at least 22 game px tall (44 CSS px at 2×), with air between them.
- **Rename:** "play again" becomes `continue` (it goes to the title).
- **What leaves the screen:** only `continue`, Enter, or pad A, after about 2.5 s. Taps elsewhere do nothing, and the receipt stays after a share.
- **Distinct hashes:** the four fake git hashes come out visibly different (mix the bits per index).
- **Unlocks on the receipt:** an `UNLOCKED: …` line on the receipt itself, not only as a queued toast.

### F3. Patch picks must be deliberate

A confirm counts only if it is a fresh press that began after the pick opened:
- **Mouse:** a mousedown that starts after it opened.
- **Pad:** A after a navigation press, or after A has been released for at least 0.4 s.
- **Touch:** the card highlights on touch-down and commits on release over the same card.

The existing 0.6 s lock stays.

### F4. Touch near-misses

- **Pause:** an explicit framed `[ resume ]` button. Taps that miss every control do nothing.
- **Game over:** framed `[ retry … ]` and `[ quit to title ]` buttons.
- **Chips:** `CB.Screens.toggles.hit(x, y, y0, pad)` gains an optional padding argument. Title and pause pass `pad = 3` on touch.

### F5. Gentle resume

After unpausing, the world runs at 0.3× ramping to 1× over 0.6 s, and Clawd gets 0.6 s of mercy (the hurt grace). This is simulation-safe because it applies only after a pause, so bot runs are unaffected.

### F6. Pad on pause

The first navigation press lights chip 0 without moving.

### F8. No stray crosshair on cards

On touch and pad, don't draw the stick crosshair during `stageIntro`, `bossWarn` and `bossDead` (`play.js` draw). It overprints the intro card (seen at 844×390).

### F7. Small extras

- Q on game over needs the same double press as on pause.
- The stage-intro card holds its punchline at least 0.6 s before fading (lengthen `introT` for a first visit only if needed).
- Fix the warning card leaking the HUD under the hazard tape.

## H: HUD and in-fight feedback

Owns:
- `src/scenes/hud.js`, `src/game/fx.js`, `src/game/player.js`, `src/game/compact.js`, `src/core/audio.js`, `src/game/pickups.js`, `src/game/hints.js`
- `src/game/bosses/memoryLeakBoss.js` and `src/game/bosses/mergeConflict.js` (draw code only)
- their tests: `hud`/`draw.world`, `fx.juice`, `player.look`, `compact`, `audio`, `pickups`, `hints`, and the two boss tests' draw cases

### H1. Text pops don't pile up

- **Lanes:** a new pop that would overlap a live one (within about 9 px in y and an overlapping x range) moves up a lane.
- **Priority:** `last heart!`, `+heart` and `perfect` beat quips (no hurt quip on the tick the hearts reach 1).
- **Off Clawd:** pops never sit on Clawd's own box.
- **Prompts stay in the arena:** the /compact keycap (`compact.drawPrompt`) and the "✻ Thinking..." bubble are clamped inside the arena (y at least `ARENA.y + 2`), and flip below Clawd near the top wall.
- **Quiet wave starts:** no idle bubble in the first 2 s of a wave.

### H2. Readable meter and pip

- The /compact meter track is visible when empty (at least 3:1 against the strip) and 4 px tall, with a tiny `/c` tag that lights when ready.
- The low-health pop becomes `last heart!`, and the hotfix pickup text becomes `+15 /compact` instead of `+15 ctx`.

### H3. Low health you can see on every stage

- The vignette becomes a stronger 5–6 px edge. On the red `~/prod/` palette it uses gold or white instead of red.
- A pulsing ring under Clawd while she has 1 heart.
- Calm mode: steady, not pulsing.

### H4. Memory Leak telegraph

- Landing rings are 2 px, in a high-contrast lime or yellow, with a shrinking inner ring.
- Puddles get a bright rim (about 0.8 alpha) and a fill at 3:1 or more against the floor.
- Bug-kill decals are never in the hazard colour.
- The heap label agrees with the wall gauge.

### H5. Responsive inputs

- A 0.15 s dash input buffer: a press slightly early fires when the cooldown ends.
- A refused /compact (meter not full) or refused dash gives a small cue: a dull tick sound (rate-limited) and a 0.15 s flash of the meter outline or the dash pip.
- The bot gates must still pass, and the bot goldens must stay bit-identical if the bot never triggers the buffer. If they change, re-record them with a reason.

### H6. Boss rules on the first fight

One-shot hints through `hints.js`, using the same once-only storage:

| Boss | Hint |
|---|---|
| Loop | `only the head takes damage` |
| Leak | `keep hitting it or it heals` |
| Conflict | `kill one twin, then finish the other in 6 s` |

- **Placement:** the hint banner moves so it never covers the boss. Use the bottom of the arena (above the touch controls), or pick per boss.
- **Compact hint:** it no longer fires in the first seconds of boss 1, since every boss starts with a full meter. It fires when the meter first fills during a wave.
- **Blocked shots:** Loop body hits get a clearer "blocked" cue: a bigger grey clink spark and a rate-limited `blocked` pop.

### H7. /compact ready on touch

On touch, show a small `/c ready` marker over Clawd (the keycap's place) instead of nothing.

### H8. Merge Conflict labels

The HEAD/incoming labels are readable (alpha about 0.5 and off the wall).

## M: menus, pad and prompts

Owns:
- `src/scenes/title.js`, `src/core/prompts.js`
- `tests/draw.title.test.js`, `tests/prompts.test.js`, `tests/daily.test.js` (title cases only), the title cases of `tests/menus.devices.test.js`

### M1. Pad on the title

- The D-pad or stick moves a gold focus over hat ← START → modes → chips.
- A activates the focused item. Start begins a normal run.
- The press that switches the device from kbm to pad is swallowed: it only wakes the pad.
- A dim `D-pad: options` hint.

### M2. Prompts

**One pad legend** for title and pause, naming RT:
- line 1: `L-stick move · R-stick aim · RT fire`
- line 2: `A/LB dash · RB/B /compact · Start pause`

**Keyboard pause legend:** `Space/Shift dash · E /compact (meter full)`. Every string fits.

### M3. Touch near-misses on the title

- Chips, mode buttons and the hat arrows get 3 px of invisible padding on touch (using `toggles.hit(..., pad)` from F4).
- A tap that misses every control by a few px resolves to the nearest control within 6 px instead of starting.
- A tap anywhere else still starts.

### M4. New-unlock pips and a readable teaser

- A small gold `new` pip on freshly unlocked mode buttons until first used (stored with `CB.Save` under `seenModes`).
- Locked teaser text becomes `C.grey` instead of `greyDark` (contrast), and a locked poke shows only the toast (not the description too).

### M5. Install button

- When `CB.Install && CB.Install.kind()` is truthy, a small framed `install` button sits at the title's top-right (clear of the logo and the bugs).
- `kind 'prompt'` calls `CB.Install.prompt()` and shows a toast with the outcome; `'ios'` calls `CB.Install.showIosHelp()`.
- The button is hidden when standalone.
- Pad: it's part of the focus ring.

## Contracts between builders

- `CB.Install` (T) is used guarded by M5.
- `CB.Screens.toggles.hit(x, y, y0, pad)` (F) is used by M3. Until F's change lands, the extra argument is simply ignored, so M must not depend on it in tests.
- New Prompts keys (M) are used by F guarded, with literal fallbacks.
- `CB.Save` keys: `touchTaught` (T), `seenModes` (M), plus the existing `hints`.
