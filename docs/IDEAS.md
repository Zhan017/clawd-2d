# Ideas for later

Clawd vs Bugs is finished as **v1.0**. This is the backlog in case we come back to it. Nothing here is required. Some items come from the 2026-10-14 UX audit ("nice to have"), and some are content ideas from planning.

## More game

- **Patch pack:**
  - **Rubber Duck:** a duck orbits Clawd and shoots alongside.
  - **Garbage Collector:** kills slowly heal you.
  - **Retry with Backoff:** missed shots curve back around.
- **On-call mode:** endless waves that escalate, unlocked after the first win. See how long you last.
- **A fifth boss:**
  - **Flaky Test:** flips between hittable and invincible on a visible coin toss.
  - **Deadlock:** two locks that each wait for the other.
  - **Stack Overflow:** a tower of stack frames that keeps growing.
  - A secret **Legacy Code** boss after a full clear.

## Polish backlog (from the UX audit)

- **Contact push-out:** a contact hit can cost several hearts while Clawd still overlaps a boss. Nudge her away from the culprit on a hit.
- **Say what hurt you:** flash the culprit, and add a "hit by …" line, plus how close the boss was, on the game-over card.
- **Hurt blink:** keep Clawd faintly visible on the blink's off frames, so the player can steer out.
- **Bullet shapes for colour-blind players:** give enemy bullets a shape distinct from Clawd's sparks (for example a hollow ring), not just a different hue.
- **Off-by-One tell:** a bigger cue than two 1×2 px eyes for its 0.4 s burst warning.
- **Faster retry loop:** allow skipping the last part of the death slow-mo and the retry boss warning.
- **Patch-pick pips:** show a stack count ("1/2", "MAX") and make the heading bigger.
- **Touch dash:** consider a left-thumb dash (a quick flick of the move stick), so the aim thumb never has to leave its stick.
- **Controller glyphs:** read the pad id and show Cross/Circle/R1 on PlayStation and the swapped A/B on Switch pads.
- **Pad leftovers:** draw notices above the pause overlay, and let a pad button reload from the KERNEL PANIC screen.
